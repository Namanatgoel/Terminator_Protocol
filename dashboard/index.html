AI Judgment Layer

Four tiers. One chance
to get it right.

The LLM reads accumulated context at each tier. If a signal of price sensitivity exists, it authorises a discount. If not, it doesn't.

1
Silent Retry Tier 1 · No friction
Triggered on transient failures — insufficient funds, network blips. No customer contact. Schedules a quiet Redpanda retry with a delay window calibrated by the LLM.
action: silent_retry
2
WhatsApp Ping Tier 2 · Soft nudge
Repeat failure signals an addressable user. Dispatches a Razorpay UPI deep-link via WhatsApp — one tap to complete payment. No agent needed.
action: whatsapp_ping
3
AI Voice Negotiation Tier 3 · High-value recovery
The LLM reads prior context. If price sensitivity was flagged, it authorises a 10% EMI discount and triggers a Retell AI voice call. The AI negotiates on Razorpay's behalf, in real time.
action: voice_call · offer_authorized: true
4
Terminal FOMO Tier 4 · Last chance
All soft channels exhausted. An urgency-framed email and SMS hit simultaneously. After this fires, the transaction is marked hard_failed and is accounted for as lost revenue in the recovery report.
action: fomo_alert
